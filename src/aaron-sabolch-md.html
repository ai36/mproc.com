Dr. Aaron Sabolch is a Board-Certified Radiation Oncologist dedicated to providing cutting-edge, compassionate cancer care.

Dr. Sabolch earned a Bachelor of Arts from Columbia University, graduating magna cum laude and Phi Beta Kappa. Dr. Sabolch received his medical degree from the University of Michigan Medical School and completed his radiation oncology residency at the University of Michigan Health System, serving as Chief Resident. His academic background includes extensive research and contributions to numerous peer-reviewed publications.

Prior to his current role as an attending radiation oncologist at Meridian Park Radiation Oncology as well as Willamette Valley Medical Center, Dr. Sabolch was the lead radiation oncologist for head and neck cancer, cutaneous malignancies, and hematological diseases at Kaiser Permanente in Portland, where he also co-led the genitourinary malignancy task force and launched a stereotactic radiotherapy program for spinal malignancies. He previously served as an attending physician at Peninsula Cancer Center in Poulsbo, Washington.

Outside of his clinical practice, Dr. Sabolch ‘s dedi- cation to patient care extends into the community. He cofounded Tap for Hope, a nonprofit organization that arranges fundraisers for worthy causes, most recently the Children’s Cancer Association. He enjoys exploring the Pacific Northwest with his wife and two sons, spending his time hiking, tending to his pet tortoises, and pursuing outdoor adventures.
